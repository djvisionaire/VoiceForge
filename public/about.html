ABOUT VOICE FORGE

We're a team of voice actors, engineers, and directors who believe the right voice can make or break a message — so we obsess over getting it right.

Voice Forge Studios started as a two-person operation recording radio spots out of a converted closet. Since then we've grown into a full-service voice production studio, but the obsession hasn't changed: every project gets treated like it's the only one on the schedule.

We work with brands, agencies, publishers, and indie creators who need a voice that actually lands — whether that's a 15-second ad that has to grab attention in the first three words, or a 12-hour audiobook that has to hold it for chapters at a time.

Today that means pairing experienced human voice talent with modern tools — including our own AI voice preview generator — so clients can hear exactly what they're getting before they commit to a full session.
