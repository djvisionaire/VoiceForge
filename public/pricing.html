COMMON QUESTIONS
What's included in the base price?

Every package includes the voice actor's time, direction, professional studio recording, and file delivery in your requested formats. Revisions are included as noted per package.

How fast is rush delivery?

Same-day and 24–48 hour rush options are available on most packages for an added fee — select your delivery time when booking a session.

Do you offer multiple voice options?

Yes — every booking includes access to our full roster of voice actors, or you can preview AI-generated options first with the AI Voice Generator.

What if I need more revisions than included?

Additional revision rounds beyond what's included in your package can be added at a small per-round fee — just let your project manager know.
